WM-AGN v1.0.11-rc1: candidate validation
Validated 2026-10-29 UTC. Unpublished; no new DOI is assigned.

Reference: the accepted PRIVATE ApJ manuscript AAS72796R3 / AE8A42,
not the older public arXiv manuscript, and the preserved v1.0.10 release.

Outcome
The repaired workflow reproduces the accepted reported results from the
frozen historical image products. No adopted measurements, uncertainties,
classifications, reference selections, spectral slopes or physical models
were changed. The original release, paper and telescope inputs are preserved.

Limits
This is preserved-image reproduction, not a fresh raw-MIR calibration test.
Fresh MIR-to-MS conversion was not executed. The separate NGC 4258 SMA
delivery/reduction discrepancy remains unresolved and did not replace the
adopted flux. Full notebooks and unpublished paired posteriors were not run.
The final plot check reused the documented locked rendering environment,
which was not rebuilt from its OS recipe during this final check. Telescope
data, installed runtimes and private manuscript/response files are external
prerequisites, not ZIP payload. Physical/model assumptions remain conditional;
these tests do not establish their astrophysical correctness.
